EVENT SPOTLIGHT · TECHNICAL CLUB

Tech Monopoly

A fun, strategy-based team game where participants compete against each other through buying, trading, decision-making, and unexpected events. Your teamwork, quick thinking, and smart calls decide who dominates the board.

01Game StartEvery team receives the same fixed virtual money.
02Buy & TradeNavigate rounds of strategic buying and trading.
03Decision ClockLock in your move before the time runs out.
04Special EventsRandom challenges shake up the standings — stay sharp.
05Final TallyHighest net worth after 20 minutes wins.

Tech Monopoly

Team size: 2–3 members · Duration: 20 min

House Rules

  • Each team starts with a fixed amount of virtual money.
  • Rounds involve buying, trading, and quick decisions.
  • All decisions must be made within the time limit.
  • Special events can change your balance and progress.
  • No cross-team interference or collaboration unless allowed.
  • Unfair play or misconduct leads to disqualification.
  • Organizers' decision is final and binding.
